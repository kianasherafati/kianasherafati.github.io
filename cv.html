Research experience

Undergraduate thesis · October 2026

ERP Text-to-SQL with Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Amirkabir University of Technology
Supervisor: Dr. Masoumeh Taromi Rad

Designed and implemented a pipeline translating English ERP reporting questions into SQL Server T-SQL, combining dense and hybrid schema retrieval, foreign-key expansion, reranking, and schema-grounded generation.

Reranking improved macro schema-retrieval recall at 20 tables from 75.27% to 80.77% in the thesis experiments.

Added schema-aware validation and parameterized, read-only previews in Streamlit. Released reusable components and a synthetic demo.
