01 / A little about me

An engineering mind.
A human perspective.

I'm completing my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology, with 137 of 140 credits completed and my thesis as the sole remaining requirement. My GPA is 17.77 / 20.

My thesis explores retrieval-augmented Text-to-SQL for ERP reporting. Alongside my studies, I work part-time as an ERP specialist at Green Dataware and have been a teaching assistant across AI, data, systems, and software engineering courses.

I'm drawn to work that combines technical depth with practical impact. These are some of the directions I'd like to explore further:

Machine learning foundations

Databases & business intelligence

Software & human experience

Building clear interfaces and dependable systems around the people who use them.
