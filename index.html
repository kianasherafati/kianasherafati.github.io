01 / A little about me

An engineering mind.
A human perspective.

I'm pursuing a bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology. I enjoy moving between the details of an implementation and the bigger question of how someone will use it.

My projects span educational web applications, database-backed software, operating systems, and digital hardware. Alongside building, I've been a teaching assistant in linear algebra, database design, and software engineering.

I'm drawn to work that combines technical depth with practical impact. These are some of the directions I'd like to explore further:

Software & web development

Building reliable applications and connecting interfaces with the systems behind them.

Human-centered interfaces

Making digital experiences clearer, more intuitive, and useful to the people using them.

Artificial intelligence

Exploring intelligent systems and their potential in learning and everyday tools.

Networks & systems

Understanding how computers communicate and what happens beneath an application.
